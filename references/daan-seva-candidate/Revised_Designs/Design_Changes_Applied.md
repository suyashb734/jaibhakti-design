## Review checks

The rendered views and interaction checks cover the 22 source specification acceptance criteria. Final information architecture selection and operational configuration remain product and engineering decisions. The prototype does not represent a deployed app or a completed payment integration.

All names and bookings are sample data. State variants such as milestone reached, inactive and retry success represent separate scenarios. The main tabbed and section layouts use the same fixed fixture from the change brief.
