Open source specification ↗

Separate parent and date

A Nirantar Daan Seva continues until UPI cancellation. Each performed date has its own bill, certificate and videos.

Make the money clear

Disclose first and regular charges together, show next debit details, and explain retries and inactive states.

Keep evidence independent

Certificate and video readiness can differ. Expose ready assets immediately and keep every completed date discoverable.

Prioritised change register

Required changes before handoff

P0 fixes the product flow. P1 completes content, states and usability. P2 covers illustration polish. These priorities are proposed for this review. Open a finding for its evidence, required change and acceptance check.

Showing all 23 change groups

P1D01Use My Bookings at every entry point+

Required by specification · Home, Profile

Observed Home says “Bookings”; Profile says “My Orders”. The list screen already says “My Bookings”, despite its exported filename.

Change Use “My Bookings” on Home and Profile. Profile helper text: “Track your Daan Sevas, certificates and videos”. Both open the same destination. Check the longer Home label at mobile widths.

Acceptance Home and Profile reach the same My Bookings view without order terminology.

Source Specification — Entry points and page header; Content updates to current screens

P0D02Replace Chadhava with the three booking categories+

Required by specification · My Bookings

Observed The My Bookings screen has a “Chadhava Orders” control and three unbounded sections.

Change Remove Chadhava. Prototype A with Nirantar Sevas, Upcoming and Completed tabs, and B with the same three ordered sections and View all previews. Use identical records and states. Prefer A for the first prototype; select the final layout after task testing.

Acceptance Both options cover the same data, including two beneficiaries and a payment issue; older certificates remain findable.

Source Specification — My Bookings information architecture

P0D03Separate the ongoing booking from each dated seva+

Required by specification · My Bookings, Seva Status

Observed The recurring card and date rail combine overall progress with tracking for an individual date. A dedicated parent detail is not supplied.

Change Create separate Nirantar parent and occurrence detail views. Parent cards use “View Details”; dated cards use “Track Seva Details”. Keep the parent reference and occurrence reference distinct. One time sevas have no parent.

Acceptance Users can explain the difference between managing an ongoing seva and tracking one date; each dated record owns its bill and evidence.

Source Specification — Booking model; Duplication rule; Nirantar Daan Seva detail

P1D04Use Nirantar Daan Seva and a separate cadence+

Required by specification · My Bookings, Seva Status

Observed The list says “Recurring Sevas” and the date rail says “Recurring Sevas · Every Tuesday”.

Change Use “Nirantar Sevas” for the category and “Nirantar Daan Seva” for the parent type. Show Weekly, Monthly, Every Amavasya or Every Ekadashi separately, followed by a readable schedule where known.

Acceptance All four cadences have appropriate labels and real dates when confirmed; no fixed weekly assumption leaks into lunar or monthly bookings.

Source Specification — Terminology and naming; Cadence labels

P0D05Identify the beneficiary on every booking and occurrence+

Required by specification · Profile, My Bookings, Seva Status, Your details export

Observed List cards show “Rohit Sharma” without “For” or gotra; occurrence identity lacks beneficiary details. The profile gotra describes the account holder.

Change Show “For {beneficiary name} · {gotra} gotra” on every parent and occurrence card and detail. Do not substitute the account profile identity. Keep a booking reference for identical names and gotras; let names wrap.

Acceptance Two identical sevas for different beneficiaries or gotras can be distinguished from the list. Bills and historical records keep the occurrence identity.

Source Specification — Beneficiary name and gotra; Multiple booking identification

P0D06Replace the eight box plan with continuing milestone progress+

Required by specification · My Bookings, Seva Status

Observed The parent says “1 of 8 sevas completed” with eight boxes; the date rail says “1 of 8 done”. This implies a finite booking.

Change Use the configured recommended milestone, for example “4 of 7 recommended weeks completed”. Count performed occurrences only. At seven, recognise completion and show 7 of 14 when configured. Preserve the cumulative total and explain that AutoPay continues until cancelled through the UPI app.

Acceptance Reaching a target neither resets the count, stops AutoPay nor creates a milestone certificate. Nonweekly labels match their configured cadence.

Source Specification — Core product decision; Recommended milestone behavior

P0D07Make the next AutoPay charge visible+

Required by specification · My Bookings

Observed The parent shows AutoPay Active and ₹101 per seva, but no next debit date or distinction from the ₹51 introductory bill.

Change Show current AutoPay state, actual next charge amount and date when known, alongside the next eligible seva and its registration state. Retain first charge and regular charge context in parent billing details.

Acceptance A user can distinguish the next charge date from the seva date and the introductory charge from the recurring amount.

Source Specification — Nirantar Daan Seva card requirements; Pricing and AutoPay

P0D08Show only eligible registered upcoming occurrences+

Required by specification · My Bookings

Observed The current static example has one scheduled card; the supplied designs do not define projected dates, duplicates or source labels.

Change Upcoming contains only the next Registered or Scheduled occurrence per Nirantar parent plus all upcoming individual bookings. Use Nirantar or One time labels, beneficiary and gotra, full date including year, offering quantity and nearest first sorting. Unregistered future dates stay out of Upcoming.

Acceptance There is at most one upcoming occurrence per parent; payment discontinued dates are excluded; a parent and its next occurrence are intentionally allowed in separate categories.

Source Specification — Relationship rules; Upcoming occurrence card requirements

P1D09Use a tracking action for upcoming sevas+

Required by specification · My Bookings

Observed The upcoming scheduled card uses “Start Seva”.

Change Replace it with “Track Seva Details” and route to that occurrence. This replacement is specific to a booked occurrence; acquisition CTAs on Home are a separate context.

Acceptance The action opens the selected beneficiary and date, with room for the full label at narrow widths.

Source Specification — Terminology and naming; Primary action matrix

P0D10Expose independent certificate and video states+

Required by specification · My Bookings, Seva Status

Observed The completed card still says “Start Seva”; the detail combines certificates and video into one future promise. No asset viewers are supplied.

Change Keep every performed date in Completed, newest first, with actual amount and independent certificate and video readiness. Use View Seva Details while preparing, Watch Videos when available and View Certificate when ready. Add in app multi video playback and per asset Download and Share. Show the 48 hour expectation from performance; expose support if overdue.

Acceptance Certificate ready with videos preparing, videos ready with certificate preparing, multiple videos and one failed asset all work independently.

Source Specification — Completed occurrence card requirements; Certificate and video experience

P0D11Add the Nirantar parent detail and full history+

Required by specification · Seva Status

Observed The current Seva Status screen supplies a useful horizontal date rail, but no independent ongoing booking management page.

Change Add identity, cadence, progress, next eligible seva, AutoPay, beneficiary management, email and WhatsApp support, and full date based history including Discontinued records. Keep the rail as an optional navigation component with state text and a View all history destination.

Acceptance An older certificate and a discontinued date remain reachable; projected dates never look registered or completed.

Source Specification — Nirantar Daan Seva detail; Date navigation

P0D12Use actual fulfilment events in the occurrence timeline+

Required by specification · Seva Status

Observed “Seva instructions” is shown as a completed green timeline stage. There is no explicit Performed event, and certificate and video delivery are combined.

Change Use Booked, Scheduled and Performed as fulfilment stages. Move instructions into an expandable block. Add Discontinued for payment failure. Put certificate and video status in separate asset sections. Use the date scoped title “Daan Seva Details” with current status and beneficiary context.

Acceptance Reading instructions never changes fulfilment progress. Performed is explicit and evidence readiness does not change the performed state.

Source Specification — Daan Seva occurrence detail; Timeline guidance

P1D13Show rating only after performance+

Required by specification · Seva Status

Observed The supplied detail asks for a rating while the selected occurrence is still scheduled.

Change Hide rating until the occurrence is Performed. Rating can be available while certificate or videos are preparing.

Acceptance Registered and Scheduled occurrences have no rating prompt; a performed occurrence can be rated before its media arrive.

Source Specification — Rating visibility

P0D14Design retry inactive and cancelled payment states+

Required by specification · Missing design states

Observed Only AutoPay Active is shown. No payment recovery, retry deadline or UPI cancellation state is supplied.

Change Add Payment retrying, Inactive and Cancelled variants to parent cards and details. A failed payment discontinues the affected date; retries last up to 10 days. Successful retry registers the next eligible date without reviving the missed one. Inactive offers Restart Nirantar Seva; Cancelled offers Book This Seva Again. Explain UPI app cancellation. Include no pause or skip controls.

Acceptance Retry success and expiry preserve the missed date as Discontinued. Restart reviews the beneficiary and creates a new mandate when necessary.

Source Specification — Payment lifecycle; Plan payment states; Cancellation and restart

P0D15Add beneficiary editing and the locked support state+

Required by specification · Missing design states

Observed No parent beneficiary edit flow or post first seva locked state is supplied.

Change Allow name and gotra review and editing before the first performed seva. Lock self service afterwards, with WhatsApp and Email Support. Preserve historical identities and define the precise lock timestamp with operations.

Acceptance Before first performance the update applies to first and future sevas; afterwards a change request goes to support and past bills or certificates are not silently rewritten.

Source Specification — Beneficiary name and gotra; Editing rules

P0D16Explain the actual amount in each occurrence bill+

Required by specification · Seva Status

Observed The detail bill is ₹51 while the recurring card says ₹101 per seva; the introductory relationship is not explained.

Change Label the introductory occurrence bill “Amount paid ₹51 · Introductory price”. Later bills show the actual recurring amount paid. Place next expected AutoPay details in the parent; do not imply ₹51 repeats indefinitely.

Acceptance A first ₹51 bill and later ₹101, ₹121 or ₹151 bills match their actual records without contradiction.

Source Specification — Pricing and AutoPay; Detail and completion updates

P0D17Remove booking success claims before payment+

Required by specification · Payment export without recurring price, Payment export with recurring price

Observed Both exports say “We’ve booked your Dan Seva” and “Your name and gotra have been sent” while also saying “Pay to confirm”. Their green success treatment reinforces the conflict.

Change Before payment use a review and payment state, for example “Review your Daan Seva”. Reserve booking success for confirmed payment and registration. Show sent or delivered only when the relevant operational event is confirmed. Add processing and pending verification states without a success promise.

Acceptance No unpaid or unverified state claims a confirmed booking. Successful payment without confirmed registration remains pending until verified.

Source Specification — Payment lifecycle; Timeline guidance. Purchase screens are adjacent consistency fixes, outside the full checkout redesign scope.

P0D18Disclose the first and regular amounts together+

Required by specification · Home, Payment export without recurring price, Payment export with recurring price

Observed Home presents ₹51 against a struck ₹101 as an offer. The base payment export omits recurring pricing; the alternative adds only a small “₹101 autopay from next week · Cancel anytime” footer.

Change Before confirmation show “First Daan Seva ₹51 · Then ₹101 every Friday until cancelled in your UPI app” for this example, using configured amounts and cadence. Keep the disclosure prominent beside the payment action. Reconcile the offer treatment on Home with the ongoing price. Do not infer an end after a milestone.

Acceptance Both amounts, cadence and the UPI cancellation route are readable before the user confirms. One time bookings never inherit recurring disclosure.

Source Specification — Price disclosure; Confirmed product decisions. Checkout changes are limited to consistency with these product rules.

P1D19Make beneficiary review explicit outside the illustration+

Required identity with recommended form treatment · Your details export, Payment export without recurring price, Payment export with recurring price

Observed The details form says “Your name”. Payment illustrations partially cover the name and gotra; the two variants use different gotras for their sample beneficiary.

Change Use “Beneficiary name” or “Who is this seva for?” and display a plain name and gotra review block before confirmation. Carry the entered values forward unchanged. Keep them out of decorative artwork as the sole review surface.

Acceptance A booking for another family member is unambiguous; name and gotra are visible without interpreting the illustration.

Source Specification — Beneficiary name and gotra; Initial booking. Form treatment is a design recommendation for the supplied adjacent screens.

P1D20Unify Daan Seva and the content glossary+

Required by specification · My Bookings, Seva Status, Your details export, Payment export without recurring price, Payment export with recurring price

Observed HTML exports use “Dan Seva”; the PNGs use “Daan Seva”. Temple and ritual spellings also vary across the examples.

Change Use Daan Seva and Nirantar Daan Seva consistently. Agree approved temple names and ritual transliterations with content and operations, then reuse them across UI, notifications and certificates. Do not assume differing offerings in separate samples are one booking.

Acceptance No “Dan Seva”, “Recurring Sevas”, Chadhava or customer facing order terminology remains in this booking journey.

Source Specification — Terminology and naming; Content consistency

P1D21Add empty loading synchronization and error variants+

Required booking states with recommended form states · Missing design states

Observed Supplied designs show populated happy paths; the form displays Verify and an empty OTP area together, with no supplied validation or expired code states.

Change Add per category empty views, stable loading placeholders, data errors, preserved last known UPI state during sync, media loading errors and download or share feedback. For the adjacent form, design phone validation, OTP sent, invalid and expired OTP, resend and expiry states if that flow remains in scope.

Acceptance Loading does not falsely show Active or erase a ready asset. A failed video does not block a ready certificate. Form errors retain entered beneficiary information.

Source Specification — Empty loading error and edge states; Loading and synchronization. OTP variants are an adjacent recommendation.

P1D22Validate accessibility long content and small screens+

Required by specification · Home, Profile, My Bookings, Seva Status, Your details export, Payment export without recurring price, Payment export with recurring price

Observed The exports use fixed 393 by 852 containers with overflow hidden and bottom positioned actions. Some small helper text and long names need review; date states rely heavily on visual treatment.

Change Add state text, selected semantics, readable progress and meaningful video labels. Let long beneficiary, temple and offering names wrap. Check contrast and comfortable touch areas. Validate scrolling, safe areas, keyboard and text enlargement; keep the payment action and price disclosure reachable.

Acceptance No clipping at 360 and 390 widths or enlarged text; focus order follows identity, status, instructions, media, bill, rating and support. Confirm contrast in the final design rather than assuming a failure.

Source Specification — Accessibility and interaction requirements; Date rail behavior. Fixed layout observations describe the exports, not a tested production implementation.

P2D23Choose one illustration style after the flow is correct+

Design recommendation · Payment export without recurring price, Payment export with recurring price

Observed The base payment export uses flat vector artwork; the alternative combines rendered phone and temple images. Both spend substantial space on decorative delivery imagery.

Change Choose one illustration family and use it consistently. Keep payment status, beneficiary review and recurring price higher in the hierarchy. Preserve the cream, deep brown and saffron identity and reusable rounded card treatment.

Acceptance The selected variant communicates the actual current state before decorative content, with all required information visible.

Source Specification — Visual review of both payment exports; visual design system is outside the specification scope.
