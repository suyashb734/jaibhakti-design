About the mandir
Shri Gaj Mahalakshmi (2000 Year old Temple), Ujjain (MP)
Located at Nai Peth, Ujjain, Gajlaxmi Temple is dedicated to Maa Gajlaxmi, the Goddess of wealth, abundance and good fortune. Its one of the rare standalone temples where Goddess Lakshmi is explicitly seated on top of an elephant (गज पर सवार) as her primary vehicle, rather than a lotus or an owl

Believed to be around 2,000 years old, the temple Gajalakshmi is historically the goddess of royal authority, state prosperity, and sovereign power (Raj Lakshmi). King Vikramaditya’s court was legendary for its unparalleled wealth, justice, and golden-age abundance.
Worshipping Goddess Lakshmi seated atop an elephant—the ultimate ancient emblem of royalty—directly reflected King Vikramaditya’s standing as a sovereign ruler blessed with divine prosperity.

Friday (Shukrawar) is considered an auspicious day to seek her blessings for business expansion, increased earnings, financial stability and new opportunities.
A lot of Business owners and traders pray here for success in new ventures and relief from financial obstacles.

According to ancient scriptures, Kumkum and a vibrant Lal Chunari are the most beloved and essential offerings to please Maa Gajalakshmi. They are the keys to unlocking her divine energy:
The Power of Kumkum: Represents the activation of divine Shakti (cosmic energy). When offered to Maa Gajalakshmi, it invokes vibrant growth, continuous cash flow, and victory over financial obstacles.
The Grace of the Lal Chunari: Draping the Goddess of Royal Wealth in a sacred red veil acts as a powerful spiritual magnet, attracting lasting prosperity, protecting your business from losses, and bringing divine protection to your workspace.
