About the mandir
Shri Mahalakshmi (Ambabai) Mandir, Kolhapur (Maharashtra)

Shri Mahalakshmi Mandir in Kolhapur, called Ambabai by devotees, is one of the Shakti Peethas described in the Puranas. The temple is built in black stone in the Hemadpanthi style and is believed to date back over a thousand years.

The murti of Maa Mahalakshmi is carved in precious black stone and faces west. During Kiranotsav, the setting sun falls directly on the deity for a few days each year, and Navratri brings lakhs of devotees to Kolhapur.
