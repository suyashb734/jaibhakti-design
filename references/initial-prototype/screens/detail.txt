About the temple

Shri Shyam Mandir at Khatu, in Rajasthan's Sikar district, is the principal dhaam of Khatu Shyam Ji. Lakhs of devotees walk here for the Phalguna mela every year, carrying nishans that are tied at the temple.

About Khatu Shyam

Khatu Shyam Ji is Barbarik of the Mahabharata, grandson of Bhima. He offered his own head to Shri Krishna as dakshina, and Krishna blessed him to be worshipped in Kaliyuga under His own name, Shyam — Haare ka Sahara, the refuge of those who feel defeated.
