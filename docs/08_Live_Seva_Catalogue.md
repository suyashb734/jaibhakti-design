## How to use this in design work

Show the actual selected offering, temple, cadence and purchased price snapshot
across web acquisition, review/authorization, confirmation, ongoing booking and
dated history. Validate all three regular-rate examples instead of demonstrating
only the ₹101 case. Keep account owner, payer and beneficiary separate.

The web takes bookings; the proposed app shows ongoing status, registered and
performed dates, independent media readiness and payment recovery, and supports
Book More. The current app uses illustrative sample data, including Hanuman. That
fixture is not evidence of a fourth launched offer or production catalogue parity.

The live detail pages still combine automatic renewal/cancellation wording with a
promise that refers to a selected number of weeks. DEC-001/DEC-002 remain open for
actual duration, consent and legacy terms. No authenticated mandate, payment or
private booking history was exercised during this asset review.

## Provenance and maintenance

[The asset manifest](../references/live-sevas/2026-11-13/manifest.json) records each
source image URL, product page, observation/download timestamps, dimensions and
SHA256 checksum. Six assets cover an offering hero and temple photo for each
launched seva. Their source ID is SRC-LIVE-ASSETS, and immutable hashes are appended
to the reference register. The original imported files remain unchanged.

catalogue/index.html is generated from that manifest and
catalogue/page.html.template by `python3 tools/build_live_catalogue.py`. Update
source records and regenerate the page rather than editing its generated cards.
New observations belong to a new dated snapshot; do not silently rewrite these
reference images to match a later live-site update.
